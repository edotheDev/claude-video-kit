# Example clips: credits

The clips in this folder are short, silent excerpts from brand films for Arckara that were made with this kit, shared here with permission to show what the kit does. They were cropped, retimed and colour graded, with graphics added.

## Footage

- **Fast cut** (`fast-cut.webp`): 8 mm home movies scanned by J.S. McDuff and Super 8 by Life On Super 8 (Pexels License); Prelinger Archives via the Internet Archive (public domain); "Вольвокс 3" by Alexander Klepnev, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
- **Slow story** (`slow-story.webp`): "Selling as a Career" (1953), Prelinger Archives via the Internet Archive (public domain).
- **Spec sheet** (`spec-sheet.webp`): Life On Super 8 and Hữu Thịnh 79 on Pexels (Pexels License).
- **Tracking overlay** (`tracking-overlay.webp`): Pexels clips (Pexels License); "Вольвокс 3" by Alexander Klepnev, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
- **Music montage** (`music-montage.webp`): Pexels clips (Pexels License); microscopy by Alexander Klepnev and Andrei Savitsky, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons ("Деление инфузории", "Спирогира", "Большой вольвокс", "Деление стилонихии 1", "Движение лейкоцитов в крови", "Коловратка Epiphanes senta").
- **UI motion** (`ui-motion.webp`): no footage; the interface is rebuilt in HTML, and every name in it is sample data.
