# Example clips: credits

The clips in this folder are short, silent excerpts, shared by their maker to show what the kit does. They were cropped, retimed and colour graded, with graphics added.

## Story reel (`story-reel.webp`)

From a narrated story reel on Palantir, made for a personal channel. The footage and article pages are real, belong to their owners, and are used for commentary and news reporting; the full credits ship with the film.

## Anime edit (`anime-edit.webp`)

From a non commercial fan edit on a trend sound. The anime footage and manga panels belong to their studios and publishers (Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man). The gears, layouts and effects were drawn by the edit's own renderer.

## Brand films for Arckara (shared with permission)

- **UI motion** (`ui-motion.webp`): no footage; the interface is rebuilt in HTML, and every name in it is sample data.
- **Fast cut** (`fast-cut.webp`): 8 mm home movies scanned by J.S. McDuff and Super 8 by Life On Super 8 (Pexels License); Prelinger Archives via the Internet Archive (public domain); "Вольвокс 3" by Alexander Klepnev, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.
- **Slow story** (`slow-story.webp`): "Selling as a Career" (1953), Prelinger Archives via the Internet Archive (public domain).
- **Spec sheet** (`spec-sheet.webp`): Life On Super 8 and Hữu Thịnh 79 on Pexels (Pexels License).
