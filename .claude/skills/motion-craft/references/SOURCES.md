# Sources

The reference files in this folder are adapted from two MIT licensed projects:

- **motion-design-with-claude-and-codex** by Adewale (LobzyJay): https://github.com/LobzyJay/motion-design-with-claude-and-codex
  The timing and spacing doctrine, the defaults to avoid, composition for motion.
- **motion-design-skill** by LottieFiles: https://github.com/LottieFiles/motion-design-skill
  The director frameworks.

Their licence texts are in `licenses/` at the root of this repository. The house layer in `../SKILL.md` (the calm default, the film grammar, the demo video tells) is this kit's own.
